Hebrew reading fonts (frank-ruhl-libre-hebrew-var.woff2,
david-libre-hebrew-400/500/700.woff2, heebo-hebrew-var.woff2,
taamey-frank-pj-medium.woff2)

Built by scripts/hebrew-fonts.py, which names each source, checks its
SHA-256 and applies the only change made: the stem of the qamats qatan
(U+05C7) is 0.1 em longer, to tell it from the qamats. Nothing else is
redrawn. The family names carry " PJ".
